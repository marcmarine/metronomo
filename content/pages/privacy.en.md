# Metronome Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Metronome is an iPhone app that works like a mechanical metronome. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short answer: none.

## Data we collect

None. Metronome does not collect or share any personal data or usage data:

- It doesn't need an account or sign-up.
- It has no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
- It only connects to the internet to check whether a new version of the app is available. No personal data is sent in that check.
- It doesn't ask for access to the microphone, camera, contacts, location or any other data on your device.

The app stores the last tempo you chose on your iPhone, so it starts with it next time. That setting never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Crash reports from Apple

If you have agreed in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports for the app. Apple handles those reports under its own privacy policy, and we only use them to fix bugs.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above.
